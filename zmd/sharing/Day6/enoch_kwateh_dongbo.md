## Full Content

Opening: Connecting the Previous Days' Teachings

Bro. Enoch began by asking the congregation two reflective questions that tied together the entire prayer and fasting experience: "What is the theme of the prayer and fasting?" and "What is that one thing everyone has captured through the entire time or previous sharing from other speakers?"

He highlighted key takeaways from previous speakers:

- Bro. Thompson's Message: The dangers of disobedience, rebellion, and how self-idolatry is equivalent to witchcraft
- Bro. Sar Noh's Teaching: The peril of partial obedience, emphasizing that obedience should never be partial
- Bro. Gerald's Insight: In the absence of obedience, sacrifice becomes valueless

These connections established the foundation for understanding that disobedience in any form carries serious spiritual consequences.

Core Teaching: The Importance of Obeying God's Voice

The Central Truth: Consequences of Disobedience

"Whenever we do not obey the voice of God, there's always consequences," Bro. Enoch declared. This principle became the cornerstone of his message, showing that disobedience, even in seemingly small matters, can have far-reaching spiritual implications.

Biblical Examples of Disobedience and Its Consequences

Moses and the Rock (Numbers 20:8-12)

Bro. Enoch began with Moses' story, reading from Numbers 20:8-12. God instructed Moses to speak to the rock to bring forth water, but in frustration, Moses struck the rock instead. "God gave them clear instruction, but they did not obey," he explained. "Such disobedience that might seem common led them to not seeing the promise land."

This example illustrated how even leaders called by God can face consequences for disobedience, preventing them from entering into God's promises.

Naaman's Pride and Delayed Healing (2 Kings 5:8-12)

The story of Naaman, the Syrian army commander with leprosy, provided another powerful illustration. Directed to Elisha, Naaman was offended when the prophet didn't come out personally but sent instructions through a servant to wash in the Jordan River seven times.

"Because of Naaman's authority and pride, he was angry and disobeyed the word of God through the prophet," Bro. Enoch explained. "After hearing the instructions from the servant, Naaman left in rage, and there was a delay in his healing."

However, Naaman eventually humbled himself and obeyed, receiving his healing. This demonstrated that while disobedience delays blessings, repentance and obedience restore God's favor.

Jonah's Flight and Divine Pursuit

Bro. Enoch connected this to Jonah's story: "The same happened with Jonah when God told him to carry His word, and he escaped. He delayed himself by running away from God, and it is true that unless we do what God wants us to do, we will not move to the next level."

Jonah's disobedience led to a storm, being swallowed by a fish, and ultimately redirection back to God's will, showing that God pursues His disobedient servants until they fulfill His purposes.

Adam and Eve: The Original Disobedience (Genesis 3:1-19)

Going back to the beginning, Bro. Enoch reminded the congregation of Adam and Eve's disobedience: "Similar thing happened with Adam and Eve from the beginning—they disobeyed by eating from the tree, and because of that they lost their throne, their dominion, and were condemned to death since the wages of sin is death."

This foundational act of disobedience brought sin and death into the world, establishing the eternal principle that disobedience has devastating consequences.

The Call to Unconditional Obedience

Obey Even When It Seems Foolish

"Sometimes the word of God might seem foolish, but we should always obey no matter what," Bro. Enoch emphasized. This challenged believers to trust God's wisdom above their own understanding, even when His instructions appear counterintuitive.

The Spiritual Nature of God's Word

Quoting John 6:63, he declared: "The words of God are not just words—they are life." He explained that God's words are spiritual and life-giving, carrying the power to accomplish what they declare.

God's Faithfulness to His Word

"According to scripture, God searches His word to make sure that they are fulfilled or brought to pass," Bro. Enoch taught. This revealed God's commitment to ensuring His words accomplish their purposes in the lives of those who obey.

Spiritual Understanding Through Obedience

The Promise of Psalm 119:100

Making reference to the main text for the prayer and fasting, Psalm 119:100, Bro. Enoch explained: "I understand more than the ancients because I keep Your precepts." This showed that obedience to God's commands grants spiritual understanding that surpasses even the wisdom of ancient spiritual beings.

Christ's Supremacy (Colossians 2:10)

"We should also remember Colossians 2:10 which talks about Christ is the head of all principalities and powers," he continued. "If God is head over all living things, both spiritual and physical, we should not His word lightly."

Deep Revelations for the Obedient (1 Corinthians 2:9-10)

Bro. Enoch concluded this section with 1 Corinthians 2:9-10: "Even the deep things of God are revealed to us through the Spirit of God to those who obey, things that eyes had not seen or come to the minds of men."

This powerful promise showed that obedience unlocks divine revelation and spiritual understanding that the world cannot access.

Conclusion: Obedience Over Sacrifice

Don't Take God's Word Lightly

"In conclusion, we shouldn't take the word lightly; they are spirit and they are life," Bro. Enoch urged. He emphasized the spiritual nature of God's words and their life-giving power.

The Final Prayer

Bro. Enoch led the congregation in prayer: "Praying that the Lord help us to obey His word, when we understand and when we don't understand. When it sounds crazy or not, we should obey. We should not focus more on many sacrifices because the Lord does not delight in those things; Obedience is better than Sacrifices."

Key Takeaways for Application

1. Connect Previous Teachings: Review what God has been speaking through previous messages about obedience
2. Recognize Consequences: Understand that disobedience always brings spiritual consequences and delays blessings
3. Obey Completely: Follow God's instructions exactly, even when they seem foolish or inconvenient
4. Trust God's Wisdom: God's ways are higher than ours—obey even when you don't understand
5. Pursue Spiritual Understanding: Obedience unlocks deeper revelation and spiritual wisdom
6. Value Obedience Over Ritual: God delights in obedience more than religious sacrifices and performances

Prayer Focus

- Ask God to reveal areas where you've been disobedient to His voice
- Pray for grace to obey God's instructions completely, even when they seem difficult
- Repent of any pride or self-will that has led to disobedience
- Ask for spiritual understanding that comes through obedience
- Pray for courage to follow God's leading, even when it appears counterintuitive
- Surrender completely to God's will, choosing obedience over personal preferences
