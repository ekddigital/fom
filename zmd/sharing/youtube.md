

 Welcome to Fishers of Men (FOM) - Bringing Jesus to the World

At Fishers of Men, we are passionate about fulfilling the Great Commission - making disciples of all nations. Founded in 2019, our non-denominational Christian organization is dedicated to equipping believers, fostering spiritual growth, and building a strong community of faith centered on Jesus Christ.

🔥 Our Mission:
To make disciples of Jesus Christ by bringing the Gospel to the world through practical ministry, community outreach, and global missions. We fulfill this through:
• Biblical education and teaching
• Prayer and spiritual formation
• Community building and fellowship
• Ministry development and recognition
• Global missions and evangelism
• Leadership development
• Prayer support networks

📍 Who We Are:
Fishers of Men is a non-denominational Christian organization founded in 2019, committed to spiritual excellence and authentic discipleship. We believe in the transformative power of the Gospel, the authority of Scripture, and the transformative work of the Holy Spirit in every believer's life. Our global ministry reaches believers worldwide with practical ministry, community outreach, and missions.
