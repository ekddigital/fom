Full Content

Opening: The Final Step - Activating the Holy Ghost's Power

Bro. Enoch began by asking the congregation a foundational question that would set the direction for his entire message: "What is the theme of the prayer and fasting?" After receiving the response about "Living the Power of the Word of God: The Believer's Dominion," he declared his focus: activating the power of the Holy Ghost.

"In order to live in the power of the Word of God, we need to activate the power of the Holy Ghost," he emphasized. This established the crucial understanding that dominion is not automatic—it requires intentional activation through specific spiritual disciplines.

As the conclusion to seven powerful days of teaching about dominion, authority, stewardship, and faith, this final message provided the practical keys to release that power in daily life.

The Three Major Points for Activation

Drawing from Genesis 1:26-28, John 14:12, and Galatians 4:20, Bro. Enoch revealed three essential pathways to activate the Holy Spirit's power in our lives.

1. Meditation & Prayer: Finding Life in the Word

The Cardinal Foundation

"Meditation is cardinal in the process of activating the power of the Holy Ghost," Bro. Enoch declared, establishing this as the non-negotiable foundation for Holy Spirit empowerment.

Biblical Foundation for Meditation

Reading from Joshua 1:8, he emphasized: "This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success."

Acts 6:4 provided additional foundation: "But we will give ourselves continually to prayer, and to the ministry of the word." This showed the early church's commitment to both prayer and Word meditation as essential disciplines.

The Process of True Meditation

Bro. Enoch explained that meditation is not about speed-reading through entire chapters daily, weekly, or monthly. Instead, true meditation involves deep, repeated engagement with God's Word:

"You can stick on one verse and meditate on it and get the life from that word. For example, when scriptures say in John 3:16 or maybe John 1:12-13, you can just repeat those things over and over... 'I am a child of God, I am a child of God, for the Lord says those who accept me or believe in my name, I give the right to become children of God.'"

The Power of Repetitive Meditation

Through this repetitive process, revelation comes: "The more you meditate on the word, you will receive revelations from the Word. And you will start to say things like, if I am the child of God (God is the creator, He is the author of everything and God has everything)—if I am a child of God, thousands will fall on my right and ten thousands on my left but nothing will harm me."

Why Different Preachers See Different Revelations

"This is one reason why we will notice different preachers speaking about one verse or book from different perspectives beyond the obvious. That is because they have meditated and the Spirit of God is revealing new things."

The Life-Giving Nature of God's Words

Quoting John 6:63, he declared: "It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life."

"We can only get the life from the Word by meditating on it. As we meditate more, we need to pray and pray upon that word that we are meditating on. As we continue to do so, we will find the life that is in this Word. When the word becomes life, that's when transformation comes and that's when we start to walk in the power because we have come to that truth."

2. Prayer and Fasting: Positioning for Power

Building Strength to Tarry

After establishing the foundation through meditation and prayer, Bro. Enoch moved to the second key: prayer and fasting. He referenced Luke 24:49: "And, behold, I send the promise of my Father upon you: but tarry ye in the city of Jerusalem, until ye be endued with power from on high."

And Acts 1:8: "But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth."

The Seriousness of Fasting

Bro. Enoch emphasized that fasting is not casual but a serious spiritual discipline. He used Esther's example from Esther 4:16: "Go, gather together all the Jews that are present in Shushan, and fast ye for me, and neither eat nor drink three days, night or day: I also and my maidens will fast likewise; and so will I go in unto the king, which is not according to the law: and if I perish, I perish."

"We can see that Esther was embarking on a journey that was unlawful for her to do, but she did pray and fast, and from that she moved with that boldness because she did pray and fast."

Jesus' Example of Power After Fasting

Drawing from Matthew 4:1, "Then was Jesus led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil," and Luke 4:14-15, "And Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit into Galilee: and there went out a fame of him through all the region round about. And he taught in their synagogues, being glorified of all."

"Jesus, through his time of prayer and fasting, when he was done, the devil tempted him. We need to be serious when we try to pray and fast because there is always a result. When we don't get a result, then we should question ourselves."

Spiritual Warfare Reality

"When we don't have this power and go out there, it will be bad for us because the world is evil and we are fighting against spirits. So in the same way, after Jesus came from his time of prayer and fasting with power, that's how we need to walk in such power, and in that way it is the Spirit of God that will speak on our behalf and do things for us."

3. Speak and Respond with Faith: Releasing Dominion

The Power of Faith-Filled Response

The final key involved speaking and responding with faith. Bro. Enoch taught from Ecclesiastes 11:5: "As thou knowest not what is the way of the spirit, nor how the bones do grow in the womb of her that is with child: even so thou knowest not the works of God who maketh all."

And Proverbs 23:7: "For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he: Eat and drink, saith he to thee; but his heart is not with thee."

Responding with Faith

"We need to respond with faith. If you say you are healed, start to walk like a healed person and do the things that a healed person can do. Whatever a man thinketh in his heart, so is he. If you always think that at a certain period of time you get sick and you accept that, then it will always happen. If you think that you cannot make progress or cannot reach certain levels, then that's how it will be. So we need to work on ourselves."

The Power of Speaking

Bro. Enoch emphasized the necessity of combining faith response with speaking: "Additionally, we need to speak as a component to responding with faith."

He referenced James 4:7: "Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you."

Proverbs 4:18: "But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day."

Biblical Foundations for Faith Declarations

Drawing from multiple scriptures, he provided the basis for faith-filled declarations:

1 Peter 2:9: "But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light."

Isaiah 40:31: "But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint."

Deuteronomy 28:13: "And the LORD shall make thee the head, and not the tail; and thou shalt be above only, and thou shalt not be beneath; if that thou hearken unto the commandments of the LORD thy God, which I command thee this day, to observe and to do them."

"Scriptures said all of the above, so we need to speak them and confess them. The more you say them and meditate on them, they become life."

Conclusion: Powerful Faith Declarations

Bro. Enoch concluded the message by leading the congregation in powerful faith declarations, combining all three keys—meditation, fasting, and faith-filled speech:

Prayer and Declaration Time

"We want to pray, speaking these truths and responding with faith:

- My business shall flourish
- I am accepted for that job, even if it is only one person they are taking. My application will be prioritized
- I shall not die, my family shall not die, my friends shall not die—we shall live to proclaim the goodness of the Lord
- I cast sickness out of my family, out of my life now in the name of Jesus
- I will graduate; my papers are being published
- Every altar that has been speaking against me, in judgment, I condemn
- I speak to the four winds; I speak to the earth—hear the voice of the Lord
- Wherever my blessings are, I command you to come to me now"

Final Emphasis: Walking in Activated Power

This concludes the seven-day journey into "Living the Power of the Word of God: The Believer's Dominion." The message established that dominion is not automatic—it requires activation through the three essential keys: meditation and prayer, prayer and fasting, and speaking with faith.

As believers apply these principles consistently, they move from theoretical knowledge to experiential power, walking in the fullness of their God-given authority and dominion.

Key Takeaways for Application

1. Make Meditation Cardinal: Establish deep, repetitive meditation on God's Word as your foundation
2. Combine Prayer with Meditation: Don't just read—pray over the Word you're meditating on
3. Engage in Serious Fasting: Use prayer and fasting strategically to position for breakthrough
4. Respond with Faith: Act like what God's Word says about you is true
5. Speak Faith Declarations: Regularly confess God's Word over your circumstances
6. Expect Spiritual Results: Apply all three keys consistently for Holy Ghost activation

Prayer Focus

- Ask God to help you establish consistent Word meditation practices
- Pray for grace to engage in serious prayer and fasting for breakthrough
- Request boldness to speak faith-filled declarations over your circumstances
- Surrender to the process of Holy Ghost activation in your life
- Pray for the discipline to combine all three keys consistently
- Ask for supernatural results as you apply these principles
