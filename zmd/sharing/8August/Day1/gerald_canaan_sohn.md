## Full Content

Opening: The Desire for Power and Its Misuse

Power is one of the most discussed and desired aspects among believers. Everyone wants the power of Jesus Christ, yet it's often misunderstood and misused. Many leaders, including those in Africa, desire power but don't know how to wield it properly. Believers are no exception—we have the power but fail to use it effectively, leading to underperformance despite our divine potential.

The Ladder of Believer's Authority

To help us understand how to properly use our authority, we must recognize the progressive nature of spiritual growth. Like climbing a ladder, we advance through different stages of authority as we grow closer to God. Each level builds upon the previous one, requiring commitment and faithfulness.

1.  Adamic Authority: The Foundation

Our journey begins with the authority given to all humanity in Genesis 1:26-28. Created in God's image, we were entrusted with dominion over creation. This is why animals flee when they see us, and why human intelligence surpasses even advanced AI. We have authority to reproduce, subdue the earth, and govern all living creatures that move upon it.

However, this authority has limitations. God didn't grant us dominion over spiritual forces or heavenly beings. It's a foundational level that applies to everyone created in God's image, setting us apart from the rest of creation.

2.  Authority Through Knowledge of God's Word

The second level requires intentional growth through the Word of God. Hebrews 4:12 describes the Word as living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword. This authority keeps us spiritually alive and builds our faith. As John 6:63 teaches, God's words are spirit and life, and Matthew 4:4 reminds us that we live by every word that proceeds from God's mouth.

This level transforms us from passive recipients to active participants in God's kingdom. The more we immerse ourselves in Scripture, the more our authority grows.

3.  Belief-Based Authority: The Witness Authority

At this level, we become representatives of Christ Himself. Like an ambassador carrying the president's credentials, our authority comes from our connection to Jesus. When people see Christ in us and hear His name mentioned, they recognize the authority we carry.

This is a relational authority—people respond to us because they see Jesus working through us. It's not about our own strength, but about being faithful witnesses of Christ's power and love.

4.  Pentecostal Authority: The Holy Spirit's Power

When we're baptized in the Holy Spirit, we experience a dramatic transformation. Consider Peter before and after Pentecost—the same man who denied Christ became bold enough to preach to thousands. This authority brings supernatural boldness and the ability to operate in spiritual gifts.

The Holy Spirit empowers us to do what we couldn't do in our own strength, launching us into a new dimension of spiritual effectiveness.

5.  Warrior Authority: The Full Armor of God

The highest level belongs to spiritual warriors. Ephesians 6:14 calls us to put on the full armor of God so we can stand against spiritual forces. This authority goes far beyond the Adamic level—it's for those engaged in spiritual warfare.

With this authority comes the breastplate of righteousness and all the spiritual equipment needed for battle. However, accessing this level requires purity of life. We can't challenge demonic forces while living in compromise.

The Cost of Impurity: Lessons from Sceva's Sons

The story of the seven sons of Sceva (Acts 19:13-16) illustrates this principle perfectly. These men tried to cast out demons using Jesus' name without having the proper authority or purity. The demons responded, "Jesus we know, and Paul we know, but who are you?"

They lacked the "license" of purity that comes with walking in righteousness. Their garments weren't glowing with holiness, so they had no authority to operate in that realm.

Building Capacity and Staying with God

Many believers make declarations but see no results because they haven't built the necessary capacity. We must stay with God, climb the ladder step by step, and grow in our authority. The power is available, but it requires intentional development.

We can't remain powerless when God has equipped us for dominion. The authority is there, but we must actively pursue it through relationship with God, knowledge of His Word, and purity of life.

Conclusion: A Prayer for Growth

We ended with a powerful prayer asking God to help us climb this ladder of authority. We don't want to remain powerless—we want to walk in the dominion God has given us. Strengthen our faith, build our capacity, and help us grow from one level to the next.

Key Takeaways for Application

1. Recognize Your Current Level: Assess where you are on the authority ladder
2. Build Through God's Word: Immerse yourself in Scripture to grow your authority
3. Walk in Purity: Maintain righteous living to access higher levels of authority
4. Exercise What You Have: Don't wait for perfect authority—use what God has given you
5. Stay Connected to God: Regular fellowship and prayer are essential for growth
6. Become a Spiritual Warrior: Put on the full armor of God for spiritual battles

Prayer Focus
