## Full Content

Opening: The Critical Difference Between Knowledge and Encounter

In our journey through "Living the Power of the Word of God: The Believer's Dominion," we come to a foundational truth that many believers miss. While Days 1-3 have explored ladders of authority, access points of power, and weapons of dominion, today we address the essential foundation: encountering Christ Himself. Knowledge about God is important, but it's the personal encounter with Jesus that releases true dominion.

The Power of Revelation 19:11-16

The Word became flesh and dwelt among us (John 1:14), and this encounter changes everything. Revelation 19:11-16 paints a powerful picture of Jesus as the ultimate Warrior King—faithful and true, judging and making war in righteousness. His eyes are like a flame of fire, and on His head are many crowns. He has a name written that no one knows but Himself.

This is the Christ we must encounter—not just know about, but experience personally. When we truly meet Jesus, we understand that He is the Word of God made manifest, and through Him, we receive the authority to execute judgment and establish dominion.

The Isaiah 63 Encounter

Isaiah 63:1-3 describes a warrior stained with the blood of His enemies, treading the winepress alone. This is the Jesus who fights for us, who has already won the victory. But to walk in this victory, we must encounter Him personally. Many believers know this intellectually, but they haven't experienced the transformative power of meeting Jesus face to face.

The Word Became Flesh

John 1:1 establishes that "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God." This Word became flesh and dwelt among us. The power of the Word of God isn't just in reading it—it's in encountering the living Word, Jesus Christ Himself. When we encounter Jesus, we encounter the power that created the universe, the authority that defeated death, and the dominion that overcomes all spiritual forces.

Why Encounter Matters for Dominion

Without a personal encounter with Christ, our attempts at dominion become mere religious activity. We might know all the right scriptures, pray all the right prayers, and attend all the right meetings, but without the life-changing encounter with Jesus, we remain powerless. True dominion flows from relationship, not just knowledge.

The Transformative Power of Encounter

When we encounter Christ, several things happen:

1. Our perspective changes—we see ourselves and our circumstances through His eyes
2. Our authority increases—we operate from His strength, not our own
3. Our confidence grows—we know we are seated with Christ in heavenly places
4. Our effectiveness multiplies—we become vessels through which His power flows

The Danger of Secondhand Religion

Many believers today live on secondhand encounters. They know about Jesus through what others have said, but they haven't met Him personally. This leads to powerless Christianity—full of activity but empty of authority. We must move beyond hearing about Jesus to actually encountering Him.

Practical Steps to Encounter Christ

1. Create space for personal worship and prayer
2. Immerse yourself in the Word with expectation
3. Fast and pray specifically for encounter
4. Seek the Holy Spirit's revelation
5. Be willing to be transformed by what you experience

The Result: True Dominion

When we encounter Christ personally, we step into the dominion He has already won for us. We become more than conquerors, equipped to establish His kingdom purposes in our spheres of influence. The believer's dominion becomes a reality, not just a theological concept.

Conclusion: A Prayer for Encounter

We concluded with a powerful prayer asking God to grant us personal encounters with Jesus Christ. We don't want secondhand religion—we want firsthand relationship. We want to know Him intimately, experience His power personally, and walk in the dominion He has provided.

Key Takeaways for Application

1. Prioritize Personal Encounter: Make time for intimate fellowship with Jesus beyond routine activities
2. Expect Transformation: When you encounter Christ, be prepared for your life to change
3. Move from Knowledge to Experience: Don't settle for knowing about God—pursue knowing Him
4. Exercise Your Dominion: Use the authority gained through encounter to impact your world
5. Share Your Testimony: Encourage others to seek their own encounters with Christ



## Key Takeaways

- Encounter Over Knowledge: True dominion requires personal encounter with Christ, not just biblical knowledge
- The Word Became Flesh: Jesus is the living Word we must meet personally for power and authority
- Transformative Relationship: Encounter changes us from powerless religion to powerful relationship
- Practical Dominion: Personal encounter equips us to establish God's kingdom in our spheres
- Secondhand Danger: Living on others' experiences leads to powerless Christianity
