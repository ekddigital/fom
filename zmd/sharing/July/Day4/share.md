The Dangerous Progression:
• Step 1: Rejecting God's direct voice  
• Step 2: Relying on human intermediaries  
• Step 3: Elevating position/achievement above God  
• Step 4: Consulting other sources (witchcraft!)

Hidden Idols to Examine:
🔍 Academic achievements that make you ignore God's voice  
🔍 Positional authority that creates spiritual pride  
🔍 Personal desires disguised as prayer requests  
🔍 Relationships that compete with Christ for supremacy

Warning Signs of Self-Idolatry:
⚠️ Using prayer to advance personal agendas  
⚠️ Feeling too educated/positioned to need God's guidance  
⚠️ Justifying spiritual pride as confidence  
⚠️ Pointing out others' faults while ignoring your own log

The Heart-Searching Question:
💭 "What is being exalted above God in your life?"
