1.1	Excerpt
Glory to God for His faithfulness throughout July! As we step into a new month, we’re reminded that even when we are faithless, God remains faithful (2 Timothy 2:13). As we will be going through the FOM Monthly Prayer and Fasting, we encourage everyone to come with expectation — even one word from the Lord can bring life and transformation (John 6:63). Let that word become real to you. This month’s theme, “Obedience Is Better Than Sacrifice” (1 Samuel 15:22), calls us to prioritize obedience over outward acts. True understanding and spiritual growth come from keeping God’s Word (Psalm 119:100), and our ultimate duty is to fear God and keep His commandments (Ecclesiastes 12:13).
We pray that no one leaves this time of consecration the same. Just as every encounter with Jesus brought change, may this season bring renewal and deeper obedience. To those sharing the Word, seek the Lord for insight and speak what He desires for His people in this season.
