🔥 Journey of Obedience: From Justification to Transformation

“When we cease defending our sin and instead confess and obey, God’s power transforms us.” Discover how immediate repentance and faithful obedience unlock God’s protection, provision, and intimacy.

Key Takeaways:
✨ Confession precedes true transformation, not justification
✨ Biblical models: David’s repentance, Abraham’s departure, Joshua’s march
✨ Spiritual victory comes through walking by the Spirit
✨ Obedience secures blessing, deliverance, and eternal fellowship

Scripture Foundation:
📖 Genesis 12:1–5 | Joshua 6:1–5 | 1 John 5:2–5 | Romans 6:23 | John 3:16–18
