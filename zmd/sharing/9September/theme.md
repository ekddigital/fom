## 🕊️ **LIVING THE POWER OF THE WORD OF GOD: THE BELIEVER'S DOMINION**  
**Event Dates:** August 25–31, 2025

---

### 📖 **Scripture References**

**Genesis 1:26–28**  
> 26. And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.  
> 27. So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.  
> 28. And God blessed them, and God said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth, and subdue it: and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over every living thing that moveth upon the earth.

**John 14:12**  
> Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also; and greater works than these shall he do; because I go unto my Father.

**Galatians 4:20**  
> For the kingdom of God is not in word, but in power.

---

### ✨ **Inspiration**

The theme of this gathering centers on the divine authority and dominion granted to believers. Drawing from Genesis 1:26–28, we are reminded that humanity was created in God's image and entrusted with dominion over all creation. John 14:12 reinforces the believer's capacity to perform great works through faith in Christ, while Galatians 4:20 emphasizes that the kingdom of God is demonstrated through power, not just words. This event is a call to awaken and walk in that divine power and likeness.
