## Full Content

Opening: Why Do God's Representatives Live in Limitation?

As we continue our journey through "Living the Power of the Word of God: The Believer's Dominion," we confront a critical question: If we are created in God's image and given dominion over creation, why do so many believers live with limitations, struggles, setbacks, and lack? Genesis 1:26-28 reveals that God created humanity in His image specifically for dominion—a divine representation of Himself on earth.

The Ambassador Analogy

Consider an ambassador sent from one country to another. That ambassador carries the full authority and backing of their government. Whatever they declare or do carries the weight of the nation they represent. This is exactly our position as believers—we are God's ambassadors on earth, carrying His authority and representing His kingdom.

The Restoration Through Christ

Some might argue that the fall of Adam and Eve stripped us of this authority, but that's not the complete story. Jesus Christ came, died for our sins, and restored everything that was lost. Through His sacrifice, we have been given back the dominion that was originally ours. There are no excuses—Christ has paid the full price so we can walk in power again.

The Faith Barrier

One of the primary obstacles preventing believers from exercising dominion is lack of faith. In Matthew 17:20, Jesus teaches that faith as small as a mustard seed can move mountains. The problem isn't that our faith needs to be enormous; even small faith is sufficient. However, many believers struggle with even mustard-seed faith.

Building Faith Through the Word

Romans 10:17 reveals the key to growing faith: "Faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the Word of God." The more we expose ourselves to God's Word, the more our faith increases. What we give our attention to shapes our lives. Surround yourself with the Word—through reading, listening, and meditation—and watch your faith grow.

The Obedience Barrier

The second major barrier is disobedience. Joshua 1:7 commands us to be strong and courageous, obeying God's commandments so that we may prosper in all we do. Many believers want dominion but continue to disobey God's clear instructions. We live contrary to His Word while expecting His power to flow.

The Ambassador's Dilemma

Imagine an ambassador who receives clear instructions from their government but chooses to ignore them. How can they expect to carry the authority of that nation? This is precisely how many believers behave—we receive God's commands but disobey them, then wonder why we lack authority.

Practical Steps to Dominion

1. Recognize Your Identity: You are God's representative, created in His image with dominion
2. Build Your Faith: Immerse yourself in God's Word daily
3. Choose Obedience: Align your life with God's commands
4. Exercise Authority: Begin using the dominion you already possess
5. Persist in Faithfulness: Continue growing in both faith and obedience

The Result: True Dominion

When we combine faith and obedience, we step into the dominion God intended. We become effective ambassadors of His kingdom, exercising authority over circumstances, spiritual forces, and every area of life. No longer do we live in limitation—we walk in the power and dominion that is our birthright in Christ.

Conclusion: A Prayer for Faith and Obedience

We concluded with a powerful prayer asking God to strengthen our faith and grant us grace for obedience. May we recognize our identity as His ambassadors and walk in the dominion He has given us.

Key Takeaways for Application

1. Identity Recognition: Understand yourself as God's representative with divine authority
2. Faith Building: Consistently expose yourself to God's Word to increase faith
3. Obedience Priority: Make obeying God's commands a daily practice
4. Authority Exercise: Begin using your dominion in practical ways
5. Persistent Growth: Continue developing both faith and obedience



## Key Takeaways

- Divine Representatives: Created in God's image specifically for dominion and authority

- Word Immersion: Faith grows through consistent exposure to God's Word
- Obedience Essential: Disobedience blocks the flow of divine authority
- Ambassador Authority: Carry God's full backing as His representatives on earth
