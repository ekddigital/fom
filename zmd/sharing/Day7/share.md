God's Heart Behind Sacrifice:

⚠️ Animal sacrifices were temporary solutions  
⚠️ God desires mercy, not sacrifice (Hosea 6:6)  
⚠️ God wants a broken spirit and contrite heart (Psalm 51:16-17)
